Frequently Asked Questions (Victron Quattro 48/5000/70-100 Off-Grid Inverter/Charger)

What size wire and fuse do I need for the Victron Quattro 48/5000/70-100 Off-Grid Inverter/Charger (4,000W at 48V)?

Divide 4,000W by (48V × 0.95 efficiency) to get 88 Amps continuous DC draw. Multiplying by the 1.25x NEC/ABYC safety factor requires a 110-Amp Class-T or ANL fuse and 4 AWG 100% OFC Welding/Marine Copper cables (for runs up to 6 feet).

How long will a 200Ah battery run a 4,000W (48V) inverter?

At full 4,000W continuous output (88A), a 48V 200Ah LiFePO4 battery runs for 2.0 hours. At a realistic 50% load (2,000W / 44.0A), a LiFePO4 bank runs for 4.1 hours, whereas a 200Ah Lead-Acid AGM battery lasts only 1.9 hours due to Peukert voltage sag.

Why does my 4,000W inverter beep and shut off with a 'Low Voltage' alarm when my battery is at 70%?

Undersized cables (or cheap Copper-Clad Aluminum CCA wires) and loose crimps create a 1.5V to 2.5V voltage drop when you pull 88 Amps, causing the inverter terminals to drop below 10.5V even when the battery itself is full. Upgrading to 4 AWG 100% OFC Welding/Marine Copper solves this immediately.
